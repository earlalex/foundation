Cookie Settings & Preferences

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored directly by your browser to personalize and enhance your navigation experience. They help remember configuration states, active simulation modes, and cart items seamlessly across visits.

2. Cookies We Use

We utilize the following types of browser storage and cookies:

  • Essential/Functional Storage: Required to preserve your e-commerce cart, active visual themes, translation keys, and secure authentication status.
  • Performance and Caching: Managed via our Cloudflare Edge proxies and service worker `sw.js` for fast local sub-second caching.
  • Security: Used to track outbox sync failures and prevent cross-site scripting vulnerabilities.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can choose to disable cookies directly inside your browser settings or empty your LocalStorage/SessionStorage cache at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact checkout functionality and prevent persistent session state recovery.

4. Learn More

For details on user personal data handling, please refer to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
