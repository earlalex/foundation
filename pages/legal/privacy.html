Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

At Foundation, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information we receive through our single-page application and serverless platform endpoints.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when registering an account, subscribing to newsletters, or making payments via Stripe. This may include:

  • Contact Information (such as name, email address, and phone number).
  • Billing and Transaction details processed securely via Stripe.
  • Profile details and course progress preferences.

3. How We Use Information

The information we gather is used for the following primary purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and personalize our services and learning modules.
  • To process secure payments and manage billing subscriptions.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or malicious activities.

4. Security and Compliance

We implement a strict zero trust boundary and AES-GCM 256-bit HIPAA-compliant encryption where necessary to protect your records. Financial data is routed securely to Stripe, and your direct database sessions are isolated and verified via server-signed JWT tokens.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us through our dedicated Contact Page.
